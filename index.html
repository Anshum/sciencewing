Team of IISER / NSUT Alumni

Science Wing is a premier coaching institute in Uttam Nagar, New Delhi — founded by PhD scholars and alumni of IISER and NSUT to bring university-level conceptual teaching to school and entrance exam students.

With over 18 years of combined teaching experience across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology, our faculty is among the most qualified in West Delhi.

We are committed to delivering education of the highest standard at accessible fees — through our state-of-the-art hybrid platform combining offline classes with digital tools.
